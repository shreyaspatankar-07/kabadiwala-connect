## 1. Dataset Summary & Motivation

The **Kabadiwala Connect E-Waste Pricing Benchmark Dataset** is an empirical and simulated time-series dataset designed to bridge information asymmetry between informal scrap collectors (*kabadiwalas*) and authorized recyclers operating under India's **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022**.

The dataset provides localized, seasonal, and quality-audited benchmark prices for key e-waste streams across target districts of Maharashtra.

- **Primary Geographies:** Mumbai, Thane, Palghar, Pune, Nashik, Nagpur.

## 6. Data Quality, Validation & Quarantine Metrics

The dataset pipeline enforces automated multi-stage validation and outlier handling:

- **Total Ingested Observations:** 600
- **Passed Domain Validation:** 587
- **Quarantined (Validation Rules):** 13
- **Quarantined (Statistical Outliers):** 0
- **Cleaned Analytical Samples:** 587
- **Completeness Score:** 70.2%

### Validation Rules Enforced
1. **Positivity:** Strictly `price > 0`.
2. **Unit Validity:** Standardized to `kg` or `piece`.
3. **Geographic Sanity:** Bounded within India (`8.0°N - 37.5°N`, `68.0°E - 97.5°E`) and Maharashtra bounds.
4. **Weight Bounds:** Physical sanity constraints per category (e.g. CRT 5-40 kg, Battery lead-acid 3-50 kg).
5. **Temporal Integrity:** Rejection of future timestamps (`> now + 5 min`).
6. **Outlier Quarantine:** Double-filter using Interquartile Range (IQR) and Median Absolute Deviation (MAD Robust Z-score > 4.5).

## 7. Privacy, Ethical Considerations & Anonymization

In strict adherence to the project's **Data Minimization Non-Negotiable Principle**:
- **Zero PII:** No Aadhaar numbers, personal names, phone numbers, or residential addresses are ever captured or stored.
- **Salted Collector ID Hashing:** All collector identifiers are transformed using salted SHA-256 (`ANON-C-<HASH>`).
- **Spatial Grid Coarsening:** Exact GPS coordinates are snapped to a ~500m grid (`0.005°` resolution), protecting the precise locations of informal scrap shops and marginalized waste workers.
